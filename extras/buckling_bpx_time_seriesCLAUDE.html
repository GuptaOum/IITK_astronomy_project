1st Buckling

220 Myr
1.32 – 1.54 Gyr · r < 3 kpc
Abrupt σz/σR jump 0.65 → 0.77. Sharp dip in σx. Produces steep ABPX rise (Phase I). BPX full length ≈ 5.8 kpc (⅔ of 9 kpc bar). Compact symmetric quadrupolar Vz patterns.

2nd Buckling

~1 Gyr
2.0 – 3.01 Gyr · r ≈ 6 kpc
Slower σz/σR rise 0.68 → 0.72. ~30% stronger bending mode. Drives intermediate ABPX growth (Phase II). BPX full length ≈ 8.6 kpc (⅔ of 13 kpc bar). Extended asymmetric Vz patterns.

BPX Thickening Mechanisms

3 Phases
Sellwood & Gerhard (2020) · Kataria & Shen (2022)
Phase I
Buckling		 Steep ABPX jump. Violent vertical bending transfers planar → vertical energy instantly.
Phase II
2:1 Heating		 Intermediate slope. Bar orbits heated passing through vertical 2:1 resonance. Includes 2nd buckling.
Phase III
Orbit Trapping		 Gradual growth. Resonance sweeps outward, trapping new orbits into vertically extended families.

BPX ≠ Buckling Only

~20%
Higher ABPX in high-spin halos (S075 vs S000)
ABPX continues growing long after buckling ends. In secular evolution, resonant heating & orbit trapping steadily build the peanut shape. Face-on bar evolves from rectangular → farfalle with increasing halo spin. Counterrotating halos suppress the peanut in face-on profiles.
